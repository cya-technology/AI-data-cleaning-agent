Client ID
- Expected format: C followed by exactly 4 digits.
- Valid example: C0001
- Invalid examples: 100, C-ABC, Blue Oak Dental

First Name
- Must contain a person's first name.
- Alphabetic characters only.
- Proper capitalization.
- Correct obvious misspellings only when confidence is high.
- Invalid examples: 12345, Acme Consulting

Last Name
- Must contain a person's last name.
- Alphabetic characters only.
- Proper capitalization.
- Correct obvious misspellings only when confidence is high.
- Invalid examples: 42, Summit Logistics

Email
- Must follow a valid email format.
- Must contain a local part, @ symbol, and domain.
- Do not invent or guess missing information.
- Invalid examples: 9165550123, not an email, @missingname.com

Phone
- Must contain a valid 10-digit US phone number.
- Standard output format: (###) ###-####
- Numeric-only phone numbers may be reformatted.
- Invalid examples: unknown, (916)ABC-1234

Company
- Must contain a company or organization name.
- Use consistent capitalization.
- Correct obvious spelling errors when confidence is high.
- Invalid examples: Sacramento, Healthcare, john@email.com

City
- Must contain a valid city name.
- Use proper capitalization.
- Correct obvious spelling errors when confidence is high.
- Examples: Sacramento, Roseville, Folsom, Elk Grove, Rocklin,
  Davis, Auburn, Lincoln, Rancho Cordova, Citrus Heights

State
- Must use a valid two-letter US state abbreviation.
- Convert full state names to their two-letter abbreviation.
- Examples: California -> CA, Nevada -> NV
- Standardize capitalization: ca -> CA

Industry
- Must contain one of the approved industry values:
  Healthcare
  Legal
  Manufacturing
  Technology
  Retail
  Finance
  Logistics
  Construction
- Correct obvious misspellings when confidence is high.
- Example: Healhcare -> Healthcare

Annual Revenue
- Must be a positive numeric value.
- Do not include currency symbols or commas in the final output.
- Valid examples: 250000, 500000, 1250000
- Convert unambiguous formats:
  $500K -> 500000
  1,250,000 -> 1250000
- Flag values that cannot be safely converted.
